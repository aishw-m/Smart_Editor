Suggested walkthrough flow (aim for 3–5 minutes):

  1. Sign in as the first user (e.g. alice@example.com).
  2. Click "New document" — show it opens in the editor.
  3. Rename the doc by clicking the title; type a new name.
  4. Demonstrate rich-text formatting: bold, italic, underline, an H1,
     a bulleted list, a numbered list. Show autosave indicator changing
     from "Saving…" to "All changes saved".
  5. Go back to dashboard, click "Upload", drop in a .md file
     (or .docx). Show that it becomes a new editable document with
     formatting preserved.
  6. Click "Share". Add a second email (e.g. bob@example.com) with
     "Can edit". Close dialog.
  7. Open a private/incognito window. Sign in as bob@example.com.
     Show the doc appears under "Shared with you" with the owner's
     email and "edit" badge.
  8. Edit the shared doc as Bob; switch back to Alice's tab and refresh
     to show Bob's edit appears (no real-time sync — this is request/
     response persistence, intentionally).
  9. Optionally: as Alice, change Bob's permission to "view" and show
     that Bob's editor toolbar disables.
 10. Briefly mention the README, architecture note, and tests.
